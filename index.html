Who paid ads suit best

Paid ads shine when buying is quick, tracking is clear, and the loop from ad to sale is short. Check your business, then compare industries.

The three ingredients

⚡

Easy to buySomeone sees an ad and checks out a $50 item in three clicks.

🎯

Clear trackingYou can tie each sale back to the ad that caused it.

🔁

Fast feedbackResults arrive in days, so you can test, learn and shift budget quickly.

Enterprise B2B is the opposite: six-month cycles, committee approvals and big contracts. Broad paid ads there can burn money unless they’re built very carefully.
